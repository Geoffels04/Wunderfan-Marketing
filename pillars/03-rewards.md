# Pillar 03: REWARDS

## What this pillar covers
Brand partners on the platform, available redemptions,
competitions, giveaways, match tickets, exclusive experiences.

## Content angles
- Brand partner spotlights
- What's available to redeem right now
- Competition mechanics and entries
- Giveaway announcements
- Winners and social proof

## Tone
Generous and urgent for giveaways.
Aspirational but accessible for redemptions.
Always make the reward feel earned, not charity.

## Example hooks
- "Your points are worth something this weekend."
- "Two tickets to Loftus. On us. Here is how to win."
- "The most redeemed reward this month might surprise you."
