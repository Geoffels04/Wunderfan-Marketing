# Pillar 01: EARN

## What this pillar covers
How fans earn Wunderfan points. Pickems, check-ins,
watching content, engaging with partner brands,
attending events.

## Content angles
- How to earn explained simply
- Pickem previews and results
- Check-in prompts on match days
- Partner brand engagement opportunities
- Leaderboard and points updates

## Tone
Simple. One step at a time. Never overwhelming.
Make earning feel easy and fun, not like admin.

## Example hooks
- "You watched the match anyway. You may as well earn for it."
- "Pick the winner. Earn points. Simple."
- "Every check-in puts you closer to your next reward."
