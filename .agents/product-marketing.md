## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Wunderfan rewards South African sports fans for the fandom they already show. Your fandom. Your rewards.
**What it does:** A fan loyalty and rewards platform connecting SA sports fans to the teams, brands, and experiences they love. Fans earn points through pickems, check-ins, watching content, and engaging with brands, then redeem for rewards, tickets, and brand experiences.
**Product category:** Fan loyalty and rewards platform
**Product type:** Consumer mobile app
**Business model:** B2C app (free for fans), brand-funded rewards and partnerships

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** N/A — consumer product. Brand partners fund rewards.
**Decision-makers:** The fan themselves
**Primary use case:** Earning real rewards for being a sports fan — watching, predicting, showing up
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get something back for the loyalty I already show my teams
- Prove my fandom and earn bragging rights among my mates
- Turn matchday rituals (watching, predicting, attending) into points and rewards
**Use cases:**
- Pickems and predictions on rugby, football, and cricket fixtures
- Check-ins at matches and viewing venues
- Redeeming points for brand rewards, competitions, and match tickets

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** SA sports fans' loyalty has never been rewarded. They spend time, money, and emotion on their teams and get nothing back.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic loyalty programmes reward spending, not fandom
- Brand campaigns talk at fans instead of with them
- Betting apps monetise fandom but give nothing back to the fan identity
**What it costs them:** Money on tickets, DStv, data, and merch with zero return; feeling like a wallet, not a fan.
**Emotional tension:** "I've supported this team my whole life and nobody has ever said thank you."

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** No established SA fan-rewards platform — the category is open.
**Secondary:** Retail loyalty programmes — fall short because they reward purchases, not fandom.
**Indirect:** Betting apps — capture fan attention but are gambling, not reward; Wunderfan never promotes gambling.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Fans earn points just by being fans, not by spending
- Built South African first: local teams, local brands, local language
- For all SA fans — never favours one team, race group, or code
**How we do it differently:** Points for pickems, check-ins, watching, and engaging, redeemed with brands fans actually want.
**Why that's better:** The fan is the hero; the reward feels earned, not charity.
**Why customers choose us:** It is the first platform that pays their fandom back.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Sounds too good to be true, what's the catch?" | No catch: brands fund rewards to reach real fans. You earn by doing what you already do. |
| "Is this a betting app?" | No. No gambling, ever. Points come from fandom, not stakes. |
| "Another app on my phone?" | One app that turns every match you already watch into points. |

**Anti-persona:** Punters looking for betting odds; fans of no SA sport.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Fandom costs money and returns nothing.
**Pull:** Real rewards, bragging rights, community status.
**Habit:** Matchday rituals already exist; Wunderfan attaches to them.
**Anxiety:** Effort vs payoff scepticism — answered by simple earning, visible rewards.
