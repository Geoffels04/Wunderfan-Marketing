# Pillar 02: EXPERIENCE

## What this pillar covers
Match recaps, fixture build-up, team and player content,
fan moments, sport news relevant to Wunderfan users.

## Content angles
- Weekend match recaps
- Fixture previews and schedules
- Build-up content Wednesday and Thursday
- Fan reaction and community moments
- Player and team spotlights

## Tone
Immediate and energetic for recaps.
Anticipation and tension for build-up.
Always from the fan's perspective, never a broadcaster.

## Example hooks
- "That second half though."
- "Weekend fixtures are locked in. Who are you backing?"
- "The Bulls don't lose at home. Remind them this weekend."
