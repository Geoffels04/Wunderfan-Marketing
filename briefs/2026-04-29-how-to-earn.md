## Content Direction
- Open with the hook that reframes watching sport
  as something that pays you back
- Break down the three simplest ways to earn
  in plain language
  1. Watch matches
  2. Enter pickems
  3. Check in at events
- Make it feel like the easiest decision
  they will make today
- Close with the download CTA

## Notes
Keep it to three steps maximum.
One idea per slide if carousel.
Never make earning sound complicated.
This is the most important piece of content
we make. New fans need to understand this first.
