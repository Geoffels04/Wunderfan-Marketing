## Content Direction
- Open with the reward front and centre
- Show the points value needed to redeem
- Make it feel achievable not out of reach
- Add social proof if available
  e.g. "Most redeemed reward this week"
- Close with urgency if limited availability
- End with download CTA

## Notes
Use a real brand partner and real reward.
Never invent availability or points values.
If no live reward is available use a
"coming soon" angle to build anticipation.
Keep it aspirational but accessible.
This should make Gareth think
"I could actually get that."
