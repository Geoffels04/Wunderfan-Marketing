# PLAN.md — Wunderfan SA

## Niche
I help South African sports fans earn rewards just by being a fan,
connecting them to the teams, brands, and experiences they already love.

## Avatars

### Gareth
- Role: Die-hard Bulls and Bok supporter, late 20s/early 30s
- Location: Joburg or Pretoria
- Where he hangs out: Supersport Park, sports bars, Instagram
- What he Googles at 11pm: Weekend fixtures, Bok squad news, betting tips
- What he hates: Spending money on sport and getting nothing back
- What he wishes existed: Something that rewards him for showing up
- How he measures success: The result, the atmosphere, bragging rights

### Brian
- Role: Bulls fan, Bok supporter, Chiefs or Pirates follower, mid 20s/early 30s
- Location: Soweto or Tshwane
- Where he hangs out: Home, local tavern, Instagram, TikTok
- What he Googles at 11pm: Match highlights, player stats, competitions
- What he hates: Brands that talk at him, not to him
- What he wishes existed: A platform where being a fan means something
- How he measures success: Community, bragging rights, winning something

## Pillars

1. EARN — Ways to earn points, pickems, check-ins, how the platform works
2. EXPERIENCE — Match recaps, fixtures, build-up content, team and player news
3. REWARDS — Brand spotlights, redemptions, competitions, giveaways

## Promise
If you follow Wunderfan, you will never watch sport the same way again.
Your fandom earns you something real.

## Tagline
Your fandom. Your rewards.

## Channels

### Organic
- Instagram: Brand building, community, recaps, competitions, carousels
- TikTok: Reach and discovery, short-form highlights, fan moments, trends

### Paid
- Objective: App downloads
- Primary platform: Meta (Instagram + Facebook) for Gareth
- Secondary platform: TikTok Ads for Brian
- Creative strategy: Organic-first. Best performing organic content
  gets boosted. No cold ad creative until week 8.
- CTA on all paid: Download Wunderfan. Start earning today.

## Cadence
- Format: Standard (1 core asset + 2 atomic per week)
- Core asset day: Friday (weekend build-up)
- Floor (bad week): 1 post minimum, always fixture or recap related
- Ceiling: Don't exceed 5 posts per week until day 90

## Weekly Rhythm
- Mon/Tue: Weekend recap + top community picks
- Wed/Thu: Fixture build-up + pickem prompt
- Fri: Giveaway, brand redemption spotlight, or competition post

## North Star Metric
App downloads. Every piece of content is measured against
whether it moves someone closer to downloading the app.
